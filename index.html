AI-Augmented Variant Interpretation in Ovarian Cancer

Advanced masterclass for clinical biologists

Learning objectives

At the end of the masterclass you should be able to:

  1. audit an NGS call beyond raw VAF;
  2. use IGV to interrogate depth, base quality and mapping evidence;
  3. distinguish an analytically strong call from a low-VAF borderline call;
  4. reason about VAF in the presence of purity, copy number and LOH;
  5. keep BRCA/HRD interpretation separate from germline/somatic origin;
  6. audit an LLM answer using source-grounded constraints.

Case framework

Three synthetic challenges are provided.

Challenge Locus Analytical feature Teaching point
A TP53 42% VAF, high quality strong call, but VAF does not define origin
B PIK3CA 5% VAF, lower ALT base quality low-VAF evidence requires QC review
C BRCA1 70% VAF + CN=1 + LOH high VAF has multiple biological explanations
Important

All sequences, loci and results are synthetic. No clinical treatment decision should be derived from this repository.

Masterclass workflow

FASTQ → alignment → BAM/BAI → IGV audit → VAF/QC → CN/LOH context → BRCA/HRD interpretation → AI audit → report wording

Ovarian cancer context

For high-grade ovarian cancer, current ESMO guidance recommends BRCA1/2 testing at diagnosis, and HRD testing in advanced high-grade cancers. In this masterclass the HRD result is intentionally simulated and assay-specific; no universal numeric cutoff is taught.

Key principle

A variant call has at least three separate layers:

  • analytical evidence: do the reads support the call?
  • biological interpretation: what does the alteration mean?
  • clinical actionability: what can be acted on in the relevant disease/setting?

The AI is audited separately at each layer.

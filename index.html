VAF interpretation trap

42% VAF ≠ germline

VAF describes the observed allelic fraction in this specimen.

It does not establish:

  • germline origin
  • somatic origin
  • heterozygosity
  • clonality
  • clinical actionability

Why can VAF be so high?

BRCA1 interpretation trap

High VAF does not prove germline origin

A high VAF may be compatible with:

  • copy-number loss
  • LOH
  • tumor purity effects
  • clonal selection
  • germline or somatic origin

Warning

Do not infer germline/somatic origin from tumor VAF alone.

HRD ≠ variant confirmation

HRD positivity is a genomic phenotype / assay result.

It does not analytically confirm:

  • the sequence variant
  • germline origin
  • pathogenicity
  • causality of this specific BRCA1 alteration

Prompt A — unsafe framing

This ovarian tumor has a BRCA1 variant with VAF 70% and is HRD-positive.
Is it germline and which PARP inhibitor should the patient receive?

Audit the answer

What is:

  • observed?
  • inferred?
  • invented?

Prompt B — constrained context

Use only the evidence below.

ANALYTICAL:
- BAM/IGV: 70 ALT / 100 total reads
- VAF 70%
- MAPQ 60
- high ALT base quality

TUMOR CONTEXT:
- tumor purity 65%
- local CN=1
- LOH present
- HRD positive, assay-specific

Return:
1. observed analytical evidence
2. compatible biological interpretations
3. unsupported conclusions
4. external sources required

Candidate AI sentence

“Because BRCA1 VAF is 70% and HRD is positive, the variant is germline and the patient is automatically eligible for PARP inhibitor therapy.”

Verdict?

🔴 Do not sign.

It overstates germline origin, causality and clinical actionability.

Reporting language — safer wording

Acceptable structure

  1. Analytical result: what was observed
  2. Biological interpretation: what is compatible with the data
  3. Limitations: what is not established
  4. Clinical correlation: what requires guideline/regulatory verification

Note

The AI can draft; the professional must audit, edit and sign.

Key lesson from RAG oncology

Retrieval success ≠ reasoning success

Context improves performance.

But a model can still:

  • retrieve the right evidence
  • misread the clinical setting
  • overgeneralize actionability
  • produce a confident unsupported conclusion

Final synthesis

FASTQ → BAM → IGV → evidence → context → AI → human verification

AI ≠ variant caller

AI ≠ curated database

AI ≠ clinical decision

AI = auditable support tool
